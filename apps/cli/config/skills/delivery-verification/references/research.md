# Research acceptance

- Confirm every material current or externally verifiable claim is supported by a direct source, with primary or authoritative sources preferred.
- Open the cited pages and verify that each citation supports the nearby claim. Check publication dates and event dates where recency matters.
- Separate sourced facts from analysis and label inference. Resolve meaningful source disagreement or report it instead of silently selecting one account.
- Check names, figures, units, dates, quotations, and links against the sources after the final edit. Keep quotations within applicable limits.
- Remove unsupported claims, repair citations, and repeat the claim-to-source pass until the report is grounded.

Acceptance requires actionable conclusions whose material facts can be traced to working direct sources. Search snippets alone must be identified as such.
