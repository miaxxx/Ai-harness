# Image acceptance

- Open every final image at its native resolution. Confirm dimensions, color mode, transparency, file format, compression, and background treatment match the request.
- Inspect the full canvas and zoom into text, faces, edges, repeated patterns, hands, logos, and composited boundaries where generation or editing artifacts commonly appear.
- Compare required identity, copy, colors, placement, crop, and style against the supplied references. Do not accept an attractive image that changes protected content.
- Check all requested size or format variants independently; a clean master does not prove each export is correct.
- Repair or regenerate defects, then reopen and inspect the final exported files rather than relying on the generation preview.

Acceptance requires every requested image variant to be technically correct and visually free of material defects at delivery resolution.
