# Presentation acceptance

- Confirm the deck has the requested narrative, slide order, titles, speaker notes, citations, and final file format.
- Render every slide after the final edit and inspect each one for clipping, overlap, font substitution, image quality, contrast, alignment, repeated elements, and unintended empty areas.
- Check consistency across the deck without forcing every slide into one layout. Verify charts and tables against their source data and ensure labels remain legible.
- Re-render the whole deck after fixes that can affect theme, fonts, masters, or shared assets; otherwise recheck the affected slides and their neighboring transitions.

Acceptance requires a complete narrative, correct data, and a clean render of every slide. Do not claim visual quality from source structure alone.
