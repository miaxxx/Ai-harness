# Document acceptance

- Check the complete document for audience, purpose, required sections, factual consistency, terminology, links, citations, placeholders, and heading structure.
- Render office documents and layout-sensitive HTML. Inspect every rendered page at readable scale for overflow, clipping, broken tables, stranded headings, inconsistent spacing, missing glyphs, and unintended blank pages.
- Re-render after every change that can affect pagination or layout, then inspect all pages again. Text extraction alone is not layout verification.
- Confirm the final file opens in the requested format and that linked or embedded assets resolve from the delivered location.

Acceptance requires complete content plus a clean final render. If the runtime cannot render the requested format, disclose the missing visual check and do not claim layout quality.
